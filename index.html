Smart Order Management System

Smart Order System is a desktop application developed using C# WinForms (.NET Framework 4.8) and Microsoft SQL Server to simplify and improve the efficiency of customer order management for coffee shops and beverage businesses. The system provides a modern and efficient ordering process by supporting role-based access control, including Admin, Staff, and Customer roles. Customers can place orders, receive a queue number, generate a QR code, and obtain a PDF invoice. Meanwhile, staff and administrators can efficiently manage orders, customer information, and business reports.
